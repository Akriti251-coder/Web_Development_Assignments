Delicour and Dishes

Welcome to Delicour and Dishes, a place where traditional Indian flavours meet modern dining. We serve freshly prepared vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes using quality ingredients and authentic spices.

Our menu includes delicious starters, hearty main courses, refreshing beverages and tempting desserts. Whether you are visiting with family, friends or colleagues, we aim to provide a memorable dining experience.

Starters

Begin your meal with our selection of freshly prepared starters. These dishes are ideal for sharing or enjoying before the main course.

  • Paneer Tikka-Grilled cottage cheese marinated with yoghurt and aromatic Indian spices.
  • Vegetable Spring Rolls-Crispy rolls filled with seasoned vegetables and served with a dipping sauce.
  • Hara Bhara Kebab-Spinach, peas and herbs shaped into delicious vegetarian kebabs.
  • Chicken Tikka-Tender chicken pieces marinated in spices and grilled to perfection.

Main Course

Our main course menu contains traditional Indian meals prepared with fresh vegetables, aromatic spices and carefully selected ingredients.

  • Butter Paneer Masala-Cottage cheese cooked in a rich tomato and butter-based gravy.
  • Dal Tadka-Yellow lentils tempered with garlic, cumin and aromatic spices.
  • Vegetable Biryani-Fragrant basmati rice cooked with vegetables, herbs and traditional spices.
  • Chicken Biryani-Aromatic basmati rice layered with marinated chicken and traditional biryani spices.
  • Garlic Naan-Soft Indian bread topped with garlic and herbs.

Desserts

Complete your meal with one of our traditional and modern desserts.

  • Gulab Jamun-Soft milk-solid dumplings soaked in warm sugar syrup.
  • Mango Kulfi-Creamy frozen Indian dessert prepared with mango and milk.
  • Chocolate Brownie-Warm chocolate brownie served with vanilla ice cream.
  • Rasmalai-Soft cheese dumplings served in sweet, cardamom-flavoured milk.

Beverages

  • Masala Chai-Traditional Indian tea with aromatic spices.
  • Mango Lassi-Refreshing yoghurt-based mango drink.
  • Fresh Lime Soda-Fresh lime juice served with chilled soda.
  • Cold Coffee-Chilled coffee blended with milk and ice.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

Follow this recommended sequence for the best dining experience:

  1. Start with our Paneer Tikka and a refreshing Fresh Lime Soda.
  2. Continue with our Chef's Special Butter Paneer Masala and Garlic Naan.
  3. Enjoy a portion of Vegetable Biryani as the main highlight of the meal.
  4. Finish your meal with our signature Gulab Jamun or Mango Kulfi.
  5. End the experience with a traditional cup of Masala Chai.

Important Menu Terms

Vegan
A dish prepared without meat, dairy products, eggs or other animal-derived ingredients.
Chef's Special
A dish specially selected or recommended by our chef because of its unique preparation, flavour or ingredients.
Gluten-Free
A dish prepared without ingredients containing gluten. Customers with dietary restrictions should confirm ingredients with restaurant staff.
Bestseller
A popular menu item that is frequently ordered by our customers.
Combo
A combination of two or more food or beverage items offered together at a special price.
