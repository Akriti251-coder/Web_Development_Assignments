Annual College Fest 2026

GLA University

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026 of GLA University. The three-day annual fest brings together students from different departments to participate in cultural, technical, sports and creative activities.

Students can participate in individual and team competitions, attend special events, interact with fellow students and showcase their creativity and skills.

Important: Registration is required for students participating in competitions and selected fest activities.

Registration Process

Students interested in participating in competitions must complete the registration process before the deadline.

Registration Deadline: 5 October 2026

  1. Read the competition details and eligibility requirements.
  2. Select the competition in which you want to participate.
  3. Open the official fest registration page.
  4. Enter your name, student ID, department and contact details.
  5. Select individual or team participation.
  6. Pay the applicable registration fee if required.
  7. Submit the registration form.
  8. Save the registration/reference number.
  9. Report to the assigned venue at the scheduled time.

Rules

All participants must follow the rules established by the GLA University Annual Fest organizing committee.

  • Mandatory: Students must carry their valid university ID card.
  • Mandatory: Participants must report to the venue before the scheduled reporting time.
  • Participants must follow instructions given by coordinators.
  • Participants must maintain discipline throughout the event.
  • Any form of unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.
  • The decision of the judging panel will be considered final.

Fest Terminology

Open Event
An event in which participants meeting the announced eligibility criteria can participate.
Team Event
A competition in which two or more participants compete together as one team.
Individual Event
A competition in which a participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of submitting participant details to officially enter an event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for organizing and managing a particular event or category.
Final Round
The concluding stage of a competition in which shortlisted participants compete for the final positions.
