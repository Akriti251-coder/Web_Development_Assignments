Academic Rules

Mandatory: Students must maintain the required attendance percentage and follow all departmental guidelines.

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Students must carry their college ID card.
  • Mobile phones must be kept on silent during lectures.
  • Students must submit assignments before the deadline.
  • Students must follow laboratory safety instructions.
  • Students must maintain discipline inside classrooms.

Important Academic Notice: Internal assessments, practical examinations and assignment submissions must be completed according to the academic calendar.

Academic Terminology

Lecture
A classroom session where the teacher explains theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or coding activities.
Tutorial
A smaller academic session focused on solving problems and clearing doubts.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments or activities.
Practical
A hands-on examination or activity used to evaluate practical knowledge.

Sequence Before a Practical Class

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Understand the objective of the experiment.
  3. Prepare the required theory.
  4. Collect the required equipment or software.
  5. Perform the practical activity.
  6. Record the observations and results.
  7. Submit the practical record.
