University Admission Handbook

Welcome to the official admission handbook of G.L.A. University. This handbook provides important information about eligibility, application procedures, required documents, admission terms, fees and contact details.

Admission Overview

G.L.A. University welcomes applications from students who wish to pursue undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, computer science, management and other professional fields.

Admissions are conducted through an application, document verification and merit-based selection process. Students should carefully read all eligibility conditions and submit their applications before the specified deadline.

Application Deadline: 30 September 2026

Eligibility

Candidates must satisfy the minimum academic requirements for the program they wish to join. The eligibility requirements may vary according to the selected course.

  • Candidates must have completed the required qualifying examination from a recognized board or institution.
  • Applicants must meet the minimum percentage or grade requirement specified for their chosen program.
  • Candidates applying to technical programs may need to satisfy additional subject requirements.
  • Applicants must provide valid and authentic academic information.

Application Process

The following steps must be completed in the given sequence:

  1. Visit the official university admission website and read the admission guidelines.
  2. Create a new applicant account using a valid email address and mobile number.
  3. Select the desired program and carefully enter personal, academic and contact information.
  4. Upload the required documents in the specified format and file size.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee through the available online payment method.
  6. Review the complete application form and correct any errors before submission.
  7. Submit the application form before the admission deadline.
  8. Download or print the application confirmation for future reference.
  9. Attend counselling or document verification if shortlisted.
  10. Complete the final admission and registration formalities after receiving the admission offer.

Required Documents

The following documents should be kept ready while completing the admission process. Their order is not important.

  • Class 10 certificate or mark sheet
  • Class 12 certificate or mark sheet
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Government-issued identity proof
  • Proof of date of birth
  • Transfer certificate, if applicable
  • Migration certificate, if applicable
  • Entrance examination scorecard, if applicable
  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • Income certificate for eligible scholarship applicants
  • Address proof

Important Terms

Eligibility
The academic and other conditions that a candidate must satisfy to apply for a particular program.
Merit List
A list of eligible applicants arranged according to their academic performance, entrance score or other selection criteria.
Counselling
A process in which shortlisted candidates may select or be allotted a program, campus or seat according to availability and admission rules.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on academic performance, financial need or other specified criteria.
Registration
The formal process of confirming a student's admission and recording their details with the university.
