Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account is designed for individuals who want to keep money safely while earning interest on their balance.

  • Suitable for personal savings
  • Easy access to funds
  • Interest may be earned on eligible balances
  • Debit card may be provided

Current Account

A current account is generally designed for businesses and customers who need frequent transactions.

  • Suitable for business transactions
  • Frequent deposits and withdrawals
  • Digital banking facilities
  • Transaction limits may apply

Account Notice

Account features, minimum balance requirements, charges and interest rates can vary between financial institutions. Always read the official terms before opening an account.

Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows customers to make payments using available funds in their linked bank account.

Credit Card

A credit card provides access to a predetermined credit limit. The amount used generally needs to be repaid according to the card's billing terms.

Paying the full outstanding balance by the due date can help avoid applicable interest charges on purchases, subject to the card's terms and conditions.

Responsible Card Usage

  • Pay bills before the due date.
  • Keep track of your spending.
  • Avoid unnecessary borrowing.
  • Understand interest and late-payment charges.

Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan can provide funds for eligible personal expenses. Approval depends on the lender's eligibility requirements and assessment.

Home Loan

A home loan is generally used to finance the purchase, construction or improvement of a residential property.

Education Loan

An education loan may help eligible students finance approved educational expenses according to the lender's terms.

Loan Disclaimer

Loan approval, interest rates, fees, eligibility and repayment terms vary by lender. Borrow only what you can reasonably repay.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on borrowed money or earned on eligible deposits over a specified period.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment is a regular payment made towards repayment of certain loans.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed or invested before applicable interest or returns.
Tenure
The period over which a loan or financial product remains active or is scheduled to be repaid.

Example: Loan Principal = ₹5,00,000   |   Interest = 8%   |   Tenure = 5 years

Responsible Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Keep an emergency fund when possible.
  • Pay bills and loan instalments on time.
  • Compare fees and interest rates before choosing products.
  • Avoid borrowing more than you can comfortably repay.
  • Review your bank statements regularly.
  • Protect your passwords, PINs and banking information.
  • Be careful with unknown financial offers and links.
  • Understand all terms and charges before signing an agreement.
  • Set financial goals and review them regularly.

Security Notice

Never share your OTP, PIN, password or other confidential banking credentials with another person.

Steps to Open an Account

  1. Choose the type of account you need.
  2. Compare features, charges and requirements.
  3. Check the eligibility criteria.
  4. Prepare the required identification documents.
  5. Complete the application form.
  6. Complete the required verification process.
  7. Read and accept the account terms and conditions.
  8. Receive confirmation and account details.

Basic Financial Example

Simple Interest Example

Simple Interest can be represented using the formula:

SI = P × R × T ÷ 100

If P = ₹10,000, R = 5% and T = 2 years:

SI = ₹10,000 × 5 × 2 ÷ 100 = ₹1,000

Total Amount = Principal + Interest = ₹11,000
