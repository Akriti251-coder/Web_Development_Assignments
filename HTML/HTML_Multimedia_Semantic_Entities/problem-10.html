Welcome to TechFuture Expo 2026

TechFuture Expo 2026 is a large technology and career exhibition designed for students, graduates, job seekers and technology enthusiasts.

Visitors can explore companies, attend workshops, meet industry speakers, learn about internships and discover career opportunities in the technology sector.

The expo covers areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Web Development, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Data Science and Software Engineering.

"The future belongs to those who keep learning, adapting and creating."

Organizer Message

Message from the Expo Organizing Team

Career Resources

Preparation Tips

  • Prepare a professional resume.
  • Research participating companies before the event.
  • Carry multiple copies of your resume.
  • Prepare a short professional introduction.
  • Dress appropriately for company interactions.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters and speakers.
  • Attend workshops related to your career interests.
  • Follow up with professional contacts after the event.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive your event confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the expo venue.
  4. Complete attendee check-in.
  5. Explore company exhibition booths.
  6. Attend selected workshops and speaker sessions.
  7. Network with recruiters and other participants.
  8. Collect useful career resources.
  9. Follow up with companies after the expo.

Important Career Terms

Career Expo
An event where students, professionals and organizations connect to explore career and employment opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning and work experience that helps students gain practical industry knowledge.
Placement
A process through which eligible candidates may receive employment opportunities from participating organizations.
Workshop
An interactive learning session focused on a particular skill, technology or career topic.
Certification
A credential that can demonstrate completion of a course, assessment or learning program.
Networking
Building professional relationships with people who may provide knowledge, guidance or career opportunities.
