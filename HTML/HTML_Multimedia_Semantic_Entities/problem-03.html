Explore Dubai with SOTC Travel

Welcome to the SOTC Travel Destination Explorer. Dubai is a modern destination famous for its impressive architecture, shopping, beaches, desert experiences and cultural attractions.

Whether you are travelling with family, friends or as a group, Dubai offers experiences for every kind of traveller. Plan your journey and create memories that last a lifetime.

Travel Tip: Book important activities in advance during busy travel seasons.

One-Day Dubai Itinerary

Here is a sample itinerary for visitors who want to experience Dubai in one day:

  1. 8:00 AM: Breakfast and morning sightseeing.
  2. 9:30 AM: Visit Burj Khalifa.
  3. 11:30 AM: Explore Dubai Mall.
  4. 1:00 PM: Lunch at a local restaurant.
  5. 3:00 PM: Visit Palm Jumeirah.
  6. 5:00 PM: Enjoy a desert safari experience.
  7. 8:00 PM: Dinner and return to the hotel.

Travel Information

Useful Travel Terms

Check-in
The process of registering at your hotel after arrival.
Guided Tour
A sightseeing experience led by a professional guide.
Local Transport
Transportation such as metro, buses, taxis and other public services.
Travel Pass
A pass that can provide convenient access to selected transport services.

Quick Travel Tips

  • Keep your passport and important documents secure.
  • Carry sufficient drinking water during outdoor activities.
  • Check attraction timings before visiting.
  • Respect local customs and regulations.
  • Keep emergency contact information available.
