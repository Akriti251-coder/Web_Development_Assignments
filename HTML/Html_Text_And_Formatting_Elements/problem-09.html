Student Terms & Rules

Students must follow these rules to maintain a safe, disciplined and productive academic environment.

General Rules

1. Students must carry their college ID card while attending classes and other college activities.

2. Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms, laboratories and common areas.

Attendance

3. Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to be eligible for regular academic activities.

Attendance below the required percentage may result in academic restrictions.

Note: Students should inform the concerned faculty member about genuine reasons for absence.

Examination

4. Students must carry their valid ID card during examinations.

5. Students must follow all examination instructions given by the invigilator.

Late entry may not be permitted after the specified examination time.

Assignment Submission

6. Students must submit assignments before the given deadline.

Assignments submitted after the deadline may receive a penalty.

Submission Date: 10 September 2026
Students should submit their assignments through the prescribed method.

Note: Students should keep a copy of their submitted assignments for future reference.

Discipline

7. Students must behave respectfully with teachers, staff members and other students.

8. Students must not damage college property.

Violation of disciplinary rules may result in appropriate disciplinary action.

Note: Good discipline helps create a positive learning environment for everyone.

Important Notice

All students are expected to read and follow these terms and rules carefully.
