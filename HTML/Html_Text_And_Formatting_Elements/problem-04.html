Mathematics Formula Sheet

Mathematics is a branch of science that deals with numbers, quantities, shapes, patterns, and logical reasoning.

Algebra

Definition: Algebra is a branch of mathematics that uses letters and symbols to represent numbers and quantities.

Formula 1: (a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2

Formula 2: (a - b)2 = a2 - 2ab + b2

Formula 3: a2 - b2 = (a + b)(a - b)

Formula 4: (a + b + c)2 = a2 + b2 + c2 + 2ab + 2bc + 2ca

Formula 5: a1 + a2 + a3 = Sum of terms

Geometry

Definition: Geometry is a branch of mathematics that deals with shapes, sizes, angles, lines, and the properties of figures.

Formula 1: Area of a square = side2

Formula 2: Perimeter of a square = 4 × side

Formula 3: Area of a rectangle = length × breadth

Formula 4: Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height

Formula 5: Pythagoras Theorem: a2 + b2 = c2

Mensuration

Definition: Mensuration is a branch of mathematics that deals with the measurement of length, area, perimeter, surface area, and volume.

Formula 1: Area of a circle = πr2

Formula 2: Circumference of a circle = 2πr

Formula 3: Volume of a cube = side3

Formula 4: Volume of a cuboid = length × breadth × height

Formula 5: Volume of a cylinder = πr2h

Note: Superscript is used for powers such as x2 and r3, while subscript is used for lower-positioned values such as a1 and a2.
