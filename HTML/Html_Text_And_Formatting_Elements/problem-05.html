Science Notes

Science helps us understand the world around us.

Chemistry

Chemistry deals with matter and its properties.

Some important chemical formulas are:

H2O - Water
CO2 - Carbon Dioxide
O2 - Oxygen
H2SO4 - Sulphuric Acid
NaCl - Sodium Chloride

Important: An atom is the smallest unit of an element.

Physics

Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion and forces.

Common scientific expressions include:

Area = m2
Volume = m3
Speed of light = 3 × 108 m/s

Important: Force is the product of mass and acceleration.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms.

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Important: Plants use photosynthesis to prepare their food.

Quick Revision

Chemistry: Study of matter and its properties.

Physics: Study of matter, energy, motion and forces.

Biology: Study of living organisms.
