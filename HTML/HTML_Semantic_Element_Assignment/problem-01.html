Welcome to GLA University

GLA University is a higher education institution located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The university provides students with opportunities to pursue undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs in diverse academic disciplines.

Our academic environment focuses on innovation, knowledge, professional development and industry-oriented learning. Students are encouraged to participate in research, technical activities, cultural events and community initiatives.

Academic Programs

Engineering and Technology

Students can explore programs in computer science, information technology, electronics, mechanical engineering and other technology-oriented fields.

Management and Commerce

Management and commerce programs help students develop knowledge of business, finance, entrepreneurship, marketing and organizational management.

Pharmacy and Life Sciences

Programs in pharmacy and life sciences combine classroom learning with practical laboratory experiences and professional skills.

Eligibility Criteria

Admission eligibility depends on the selected program. Students should carefully check the academic requirements before submitting an application.

  • Applicants must satisfy the minimum academic qualification.
  • Required subjects must be studied in the qualifying examination.
  • Some programs may require entrance examination scores.
  • Applicants must submit valid academic and identification documents.
  • Program-specific eligibility requirements may apply.

Campus Facilities & Student Benefits

  • Modern classrooms and laboratories
  • Central library and digital learning resources
  • Computer and research facilities
  • Sports and recreational facilities
  • Student clubs and cultural activities
  • Career development and placement support
  • Industry interaction and professional development opportunities

Application Process

Follow these steps to complete the admission application:

  1. Choose the desired academic program.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Complete the online application form.
  4. Upload or provide the required information and documents.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee.
  6. Submit the application before the deadline.
  7. Check admission updates and complete further formalities.
