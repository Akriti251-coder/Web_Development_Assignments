Welcome to Jan Seva Healthcare Centre

Jan Seva Healthcare Centre is a multi-specialty healthcare facility providing accessible outpatient consultation and general healthcare services. Our centre brings together healthcare professionals from different medical departments.

We aim to provide a respectful, organized and patient-friendly healthcare experience while helping visitors find appropriate departments and appointment information.

Appointments are recommended for planned consultations so that patients can check available schedules before visiting.

Departments & Services

The centre provides access to several departments for consultations and routine healthcare needs.

  • General Medicine
  • Cardiology
  • Paediatrics
  • Dermatology
  • Orthopaedics
  • ENT Services
  • Diagnostic and laboratory support

How to Request an Appointment

Follow these steps to submit an appointment request:

  1. Review the available department and doctor schedules.
  2. Select your preferred department and specialist.
  3. Choose a suitable date and preferred time.
  4. Enter accurate patient contact information.
  5. Provide a short description of the reason for the visit.
  6. Confirm the consent declaration.
  7. Submit the appointment request.

Healthcare & Appointment Terms

Consultation
A scheduled interaction between a patient and an appropriate healthcare professional regarding the patient's healthcare needs.
Follow-up
A subsequent consultation arranged after an earlier visit when additional review may be appropriate.
OPD
OPD stands for Outpatient Department, where patients receive healthcare services without being admitted as inpatients.
Appointment
A scheduled time requested for a consultation or healthcare service.

Patient Resources

Patients are encouraged to keep relevant previous medical records and appointment information available when visiting the centre, where appropriate.

  • Carry previous prescriptions or medical records when relevant.
  • Arrive a few minutes before the scheduled appointment.
  • Provide accurate contact information.
  • Inform the reception desk about your appointment on arrival.
  • Follow the instructions provided by healthcare staff.
