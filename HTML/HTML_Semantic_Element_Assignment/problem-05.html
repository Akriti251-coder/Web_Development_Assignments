Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a three-day event designed to connect students, graduates, professionals and job seekers with universities, technology companies, financial institutions, consulting firms and training organizations.

The fair will provide opportunities to explore employment, internships, higher education, scholarships, apprenticeships and professional training.

The event will take place from 18 September to 20 September 2026. Participants can attend career talks, organization sessions, counselling meetings and skill-development workshops.

Early registration is recommended because some counselling and organization sessions have limited seating.


Event Dates: 18–20 September 2026
Opening Time: 9:00 AM
Venue: National Convention Centre, New Delhi

Objectives of the Fair

  • Connect students with employers and education providers.
  • Provide information about career pathways and industries.
  • Help job seekers understand current employment opportunities.
  • Promote internships, apprenticeships and skill development.
  • Introduce students to scholarship and higher-education options.
  • Encourage professional networking and career planning.

Preparation Guide

Preparing before attending the fair can help you make better use of your time and communicate confidently with representatives.

Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume before attending.
  • Research organizations you are interested in.
  • Prepare a short introduction about your skills and goals.
  • Carry a notebook or digital device for taking notes.
  • Prepare questions for organization representatives.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Review your academic qualifications and projects.
  • Keep copies of your resume ready for relevant opportunities.

Important Attendee Notices

Registration confirmation: Keep your registration confirmation available when entering the venue.

Arrival: Participants should arrive at least 30 minutes before their first selected session.

Professional conduct: Respect organization representatives and other participants.

Documents: Carry a valid student or professional identification document if required by the event organizers.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete the online registration form.
  2. Receive your registration confirmation.
  3. Review the organization and session schedule.
  4. Prepare your resume and questions.
  5. Arrive at the venue and complete entry verification.
  6. Attend selected organization sessions and workshops.
  7. Network with recruiters, educators and other participants.
  8. Follow up on suitable career or education opportunities.

Career Information & FAQs

Career Terminology

Career Fair
An event where employers, educational institutions and career organizations interact with potential candidates.
Internship
A temporary learning and work opportunity that allows students or graduates to gain practical experience.
Placement
A process through which an organization or institution connects eligible candidates with employment opportunities.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students to support their education.
Apprenticeship
A structured training arrangement that combines practical workplace experience with learning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend the fair?

Students, graduates, job seekers and individuals exploring further education or career opportunities can register.

Do I need a resume?

A resume is recommended for participants interested in employment or internship opportunities.

Can students explore higher education?

Yes. Education-focused sessions can provide information about academic programs, scholarships and research opportunities.
