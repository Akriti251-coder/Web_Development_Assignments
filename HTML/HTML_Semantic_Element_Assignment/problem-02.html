About OpenAI

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence company focused on researching and developing advanced AI systems. Its work brings together researchers, engineers, product specialists, designers and other professionals.

At OpenAI, employees work on challenging problems involving artificial intelligence, machine learning, software, research and product development.

Our employee-value statement is: Build useful technology while thinking carefully about its impact on people and society.

Careers at OpenAI

We look for people who enjoy solving difficult problems, collaborating across disciplines and learning continuously. Opportunities may be available across research, engineering, product, design, operations and other teams.

Software Engineer

Machine Learning Engineer

Research Scientist

Data Scientist

Our Working Environment

Hiring Process

Our hiring process is designed to understand a candidate's skills, experience, problem-solving ability and potential contribution to the team.

  1. Submit an online application.
  2. Application and resume screening.
  3. Initial conversation with the recruiting team.
  4. Technical or role-specific assessment.
  5. Interviews with relevant team members.
  6. Final review and hiring decision.
  7. Offer and onboarding process.

Hiring Stages Explained

Screening
Recruiters review the application, resume and relevant experience to determine whether the candidate matches the position.
Technical Round
Candidates may be evaluated on technical knowledge, problem solving, programming or skills relevant to the role.
HR Round
The recruiter discusses the candidate's experience, expectations, communication and role-related information.
Offer
Selected candidates receive information about the role, employment terms and the next steps for joining.
