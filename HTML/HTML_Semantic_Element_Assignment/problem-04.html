Dell Inspiron 14 Plus

A premium everyday laptop combining strong performance, a high-resolution display and long battery life in a portable design.

Product Overview

The Dell Inspiron 14 Plus is designed for users who need dependable performance without sacrificing portability. Its combination of a modern processor, fast SSD storage and high-refresh-rate display makes it suitable for productivity, programming, study and multimedia.

Important Product Terms

RAM
Random Access Memory used for temporarily storing information required by running applications.
SSD
Solid State Drive used for permanent storage of the operating system, applications and personal files.
Refresh Rate
The number of times a display refreshes its image every second, measured in Hz.
Warranty
A manufacturer's coverage period during which eligible product faults may be repaired according to warranty terms.
